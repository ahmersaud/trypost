Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Apptech Social is a social media management service operated by Apptech Private Limited. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Apptech Social.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Apptech Social, we may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, account information, content, media files, captions, scheduling information, and other information necessary to provide the service.

2. Connected Social Media Accounts

Apptech Social allows you to connect third-party social media accounts, including services such as TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, and other supported platforms.

When you connect a social media account, we may receive information authorized by you through that platform's API, such as your account identifier, display name, profile information, access tokens, refresh tokens, published content information, and analytics or statistics where you have granted the required permissions.

We only request access necessary to provide the social media management features you choose to use.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate Apptech Social;
  • connect and manage authorized social media accounts;
  • create, schedule, upload, and publish content at your request;
  • retrieve account, content, and analytics information where authorized;
  • maintain account connections and authentication;
  • send service-related notifications;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse; and
  • diagnose and improve the reliability of the service.

4. Social Media Content

Content, images, videos, captions, and other media that you provide may be stored and processed by Apptech Social so that the service can publish or schedule that content to social media platforms selected by you.

We do not publish content to a social media account unless initiated or configured by an authorized user of the service.

5. Third-Party Services

Apptech Social integrates with third-party services and APIs. These may include TikTok, Meta services such as Facebook and Instagram, Google services such as YouTube, and other social media platforms.

Your use of those platforms remains subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. Information transmitted to a third-party platform is handled by that platform according to its own policies.

6. Authentication Tokens

When you authorize a supported social media platform, Apptech Social may securely store access tokens and refresh tokens necessary to maintain the connection and perform actions you authorize.

These credentials are used only for providing the connected social media functionality and are not sold to third parties.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate Apptech Social, or with connected social media platforms when required to perform actions requested by you.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

9. Disconnecting Social Accounts

You may disconnect connected social media accounts from Apptech Social. You may also revoke Apptech Social's access through the settings provided by the applicable social media platform.

10. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your Apptech Social account by contacting us. We may retain limited information where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or compliance purposes.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information processed through Apptech Social. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to make a privacy or data-deletion request, contact Apptech Private Limited through our official website.
